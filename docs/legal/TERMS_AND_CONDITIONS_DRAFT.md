## About the service

ParaGO connects passengers, drivers and administrators for jeepney route operations. It provides pickup requests, vehicle tracking, ride updates, stop alerts and support. The service operator and whether this is a thesis pilot or public service must be confirmed before publication.

## Your account

Provide accurate account information, keep your sign-in details private and report suspected account misuse through Support. Drivers must use their assigned account and provide accurate driver and vehicle information. Do not impersonate others or access another person's account.

## Using rides and live information

Check the route, jeepney and pickup point before boarding. Pickup requests, notifications and stop alerts depend on connectivity and operational conditions. ETA is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival time; traffic, GPS accuracy, stops and departure delays can affect it. Confirm important pickup and unloading instructions with the driver.

Use the app safely. Drivers should interact with it only when safe and lawful to do so. The app is not an emergency service; seek appropriate emergency assistance when needed.

## Responsible use

Do not submit false requests, harass others, upload documents you are not authorized to provide, interfere with the service or attempt unauthorized access. Use support messaging for service-related concerns. Account restrictions may be applied for misuse through the service's account administration process; contact Support to ask about a restriction.

## Privacy and questions

The Privacy Policy explains how account, location, ride and support information is handled. Reading that notice does not itself provide consent to every form of processing. Questions about these terms can be raised through in-app Support or the operator's confirmed contact address.
