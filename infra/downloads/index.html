Install on Android

  1. Download the APK for your app.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser or file manager.
  3. Choose Install or Update, then open SakayGaray.

If installation fails, keep your existing app and contact support. Avoid uninstalling until you know you can sign in again.
